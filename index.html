About HitoCast

About HitoCast

A few years ago, one of us watched a documentary about salt. Salt built empires. Salt started wars. Salt paid Roman soldiers — that is where the word salary comes from. It was history. But it felt like a story.

And that question would not go away: what if English learners could feel that? That is how HitoCast began.

HitoCast is a small research team. We study the history of things that shaped the world — food, inventions, people, empires — and retell those stories in simple English for learners at A2 to B2 level.
