Privacy Policy

Plain words about privacy: what this website collects, what it does not, and the third-party services it uses.

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Information We Collect

HitoCast does not run user accounts, sign-ups, or contact forms. We do not ask for your name, email address, or any other personal information, and we do not collect information about you directly. We do not run analytics on this site, and we do not set cookies ourselves.

Hosting

This website is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages. When you visit, GitHub may process technical information, such as your IP address, in order to deliver the pages. How GitHub handles that information is described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Third-Party Services

Most stories on this site include a video embedded from YouTube. When you play a video, YouTube (Google) may collect information about your visit, as described in Google's Privacy Policy. This site also loads its fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser contacts Google's servers when a page loads; Google may collect technical information such as your IP address under the same policy. We use these services only because the site needs them to show videos and to display its fonts; we do not use them to follow you around the web. Links to YouTube and other websites take you to those sites, which are governed by their own privacy policies.

Cookies

We do not set cookies ourselves. YouTube embeds and Google Fonts may use cookies or similar technologies under their own policies. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings; the stories on this site will still work, although an embedded video may behave differently.

Children's Privacy

HitoCast is an educational site for English learners, and its stories are written for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, because we do not collect personal information from anyone. If you are a parent or teacher and you have a question, the way to reach us is through our YouTube channel.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this page from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top shows the latest version.

Contact

HitoCast does not publish an email address. You can reach us through our YouTube channel.

Terms of Use

The stories, images, and videos on this site belong to HitoCast unless stated otherwise. You are welcome to read, watch, and share links to them for personal learning. Please do not copy or republish the stories for commercial use without permission. Links to other websites are provided for reference; HitoCast is not responsible for the content of other sites. This site is provided as-is, for learning.
